# Competitive Research and Differentiation Hypothesis

## Purpose

This document is a research checklist, not proof that AuthorityGraph has no competitors. Re-check current products before submission because features change.

## Alternatives to investigate

### Blockaid
Known for web3 transaction security, malicious-contract detection, transaction simulation, and wallet protection.
Potential overlap: broad risk scanning.
Differentiation hypothesis: inventory of protocol-level privileged controls with evidence and repeatable review workflow.

### Sec3
Known for Solana security audits and monitoring, including nonce and multisig-related monitoring.
Potential overlap: authority and operational security monitoring.
Differentiation hypothesis: accessible, lightweight inventory for small teams before they purchase a broader security engagement.
Validation needed: determine how much of this workflow existing Sec3 products already cover.

### Squads
Known for Solana multisig and smart-account / treasury infrastructure.
Potential overlap: management of privileged actions.
Differentiation hypothesis: neutral inventory and evidence layer that can complement custody and approval tools rather than replace them.
Validation needed: understand its current security and reporting features.

### Explorers and internal scripts
Solana explorers and SDK scripts can reveal account metadata and authorities.
Potential overlap: one-off lookups.
Differentiation hypothesis: normalized cross-account reports, explicit uncertainty, change history, and team review.
Validation needed: test whether users find a separate product valuable enough to pay for.

## Questions that must be answered before claiming differentiation
- Which exact feature is missing from each competitor?
- Is the gap meaningful or merely a UI preference?
- Who experiences the problem frequently?
- How do they solve it today?
- Is the proposed workflow faster or more reliable?
- Would a team pay for it, and who approves the budget?
- What evidence supports each answer?

## Safe positioning

Use: "AuthorityGraph focuses on an evidence-backed inventory of selected direct authorities for Solana programs and token mints."

Avoid: "The first tool of its kind," "no competitors," or "all Solana permissions in one place" unless supported by current research.
