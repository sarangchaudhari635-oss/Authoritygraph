# Demo Script (2 to 3 minutes)

## 0:00 to 0:20 — Problem
"Solana teams can audit program logic and still overlook who holds critical on-chain permissions. AuthorityGraph makes selected control points visible with evidence."

## 0:20 to 0:45 — Input
"Here is a program or token mint address. AuthorityGraph reads public account data from the selected Solana cluster. It never asks for a seed phrase or signs a transaction."

## 0:45 to 1:25 — Findings
Show:
- Direct upgrade authority, or why it could not be verified
- Mint and freeze authority for a token mint
- The evidence account and observed value
- Recommended review action

Do not use fabricated results. Use a known address that you verified before recording.

## 1:25 to 1:55 — Explain limits
"An active authority is not automatically a vulnerability. This MVP does not claim to resolve every PDA or multisig relationship. Unknown layouts are marked unsupported rather than guessed."

## 1:55 to 2:25 — Customer and business
"Our initial customer is a small Solana team preparing to launch or review a protocol. We are validating whether a saved authority inventory and change alerts can become a useful recurring workflow."

Only mention interviews, usage, or traction that actually happened.

## 2:25 to 2:45 — Roadmap
"Next, we plan to add verified multisig adapters, historical snapshots, and alerts when critical authority configuration changes."

## Recording checklist
- Use a stable network and verified test addresses.
- Hide RPC keys and environment files.
- Keep browser zoom readable.
- Show the product working, not only slides.
- Mention the limitations honestly.
